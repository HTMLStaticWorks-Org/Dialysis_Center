New Patient Orientation

Your First Dialysis Session: Step by Step

Starting dialysis can feel overwhelming. Our nurses walk beside you from arrival through post-treatment recovery.

Step 1 • Arrival

Check-In & Weight Assessment

Wear comfortable, loose-sleeved clothing. Your nurse checks your pre-dialysis weight, blood pressure, and temperature to calculate gentle fluid removal.

Step 2 • Treatment

Relax in Your Heated Suite

During your 3.5 to 4-hour session, you can read, stream movies on your suite tablet, or rest while our team monitors your vitals continuously.

Step 3 • Follow-Up

Post-Session & Portal Sync

After post-weight verification, your session summary and Kt/V metrics sync automatically to your Patient Dashboard.
