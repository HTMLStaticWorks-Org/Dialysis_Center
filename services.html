Treatment Modalities

Comprehensive Dialysis & Kidney Support

Every patient's lifestyle and clinical profile is unique. We offer flexible in-center, nocturnal, and home-based renal therapies backed by continuous nephrology oversight.

3x Weekly • 3.5–4 Hours

In-Center Hemodialysis

High-flux hemodialysis using single-use biocompatible dialyzers and ultra-pure reverse osmosis water. Enjoy heated massage recliners, high-speed Wi-Fi, and 1:3 nurse-to-patient ratios.

  • Morning (6:30 AM), Midday (11:30 AM) & Afternoon (4:30 PM) shifts
  • Continuous blood pressure & venous pressure telemetry
  • On-site nephrologist rounds every week
Book Hemodialysis Shift
Home-Based • Needle-Free

Peritoneal Dialysis (CAPD & APD)

Gentle daily filtration using your body's natural peritoneal membrane. Ideal for patients seeking travel flexibility, fewer dietary restrictions, and overnight automated cycler therapy.

  • 2-week 1-on-1 home training with certified PD nurses
  • Direct monthly delivery of sterile dialysate supplies
  • 24/7 on-call PD nurse triage line
Request PD Evaluation
Lifeline Protection

Vascular Access & Doppler Care

Early detection keeps your AV fistula or graft healthy. Our access coordinators perform routine ultrasound flow mapping and coordinate seamlessly with vascular surgeons.

Schedule Access Screening
Overnight • 6–8 Hours

Nocturnal In-Center Hemodialysis

Sleep comfortably in darkened, quiet suites while receiving slower, extended dialysis. Longer sessions improve toxin clearance, reduce post-dialysis fatigue, and free up your daytime schedule.
